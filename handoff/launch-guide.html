D.RAM Demolition — Launch Guide

Netlify setup · Wix setup · copy-ready Wix content for all 32 pages. Generated October 9, 2026.

You have two versions of the same website

Netlify version — the custom-coded site already built for you: custom design, multi-step estimate form with photo upload, automatic lead scoring, confirmation emails/texts and full tracking. You change it by asking Claude or editing text files on GitHub.
Wix version — the same pages, text, SEO titles and Google data, rebuilt by you (or a Wix designer) in the Wix drag-and-drop editor using the content in the Wix page content tab. Easiest to change yourself; uses Wix's own forms and templates.
Important: only one can be live on dramdemo.com. A domain points to one website at a time, and publishing the same text on two public sites makes Google treat them as duplicates — both rank worse. Pick one to be the real site on dramdemo.com. Keep the other unpublished (Wix) or on its free test address (Netlify’s pages already tell Google the real address is dramdemo.com, so a Netlify test copy won’t compete).

Still needed from you (either version)

  • Your own job photos — the single biggest trust and ranking factor
  • Your years of demolition experience, and weekend hours if any
  • Your own story for the About page (there is factual placeholder text for now)
  • Google Business Profile and social links when ready

Put the custom site live on Netlify

Cost: Netlify’s free plan covers this site. Optional paid add-ons only if you later want lots of form submissions or team members.

A. Full setup (recommended — everything works)

  1. Go to netlify.com → Sign up → Sign up with GitHub, using the GitHub account that owns Dram2025/website-build.
  2. Click Add new site → Import an existing project → GitHub, choose website-build. For branch, pick claude/dram-demolition-website-y8kc10 (or main once you merge it). Netlify reads the build settings automatically — just click Deploy.
  3. In a minute you get a free test address like random-name.netlify.app. Open it on your phone and click around.
  4. Turn on forms: Site configuration → Forms → enable form detection, then Deploys → Trigger deploy. Under Forms → Form notifications add an email notification to david@dramdemo.com so every lead also lands in your inbox.
  5. Submit a test estimate on the live test site and confirm it shows up under Forms and in your email.
  6. Connect dramdemo.com (when you’re ready to go live): Domain management → Add a domain → dramdemo.com. Netlify shows the exact DNS records to set. If your domain was bought through Wix: in Wix go to Domains → ⋯ → Manage DNS records, and replace the A record and the www CNAME with the values Netlify shows. HTTPS turns on automatically within an hour.
  7. Optional upgrades (Site configuration → Environment variables), each switches on by itself:

B. Quick look (no account linking)

Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag in the dram-site-netlify.zip file (unzipped folder) Claude sent you. You get a live test link instantly. Forms appear in the dashboard, but the automatic emails/texts and auto-updates from GitHub need setup A.

Making changes later

  • Ask Claude (“change the hours”, “add this project with these photos”) — changes go to GitHub and Netlify republishes automatically.
  • Or edit text yourself on github.com: open the file (e.g. src/data/site.js), click the ✏️ pencil, edit, Commit changes.

FAQ

H2Common questions

Q: How much does demolition or concrete removal cost?

A: It depends on size, thickness, reinforcement, access and haul distance. We don’t quote blind: we measure on site (or price from your photos for simple jobs) and give you a free written estimate that spells out exactly what’s included.

Q: Are you a local company?

A: Yes. D.RAM is based in Fullerton and works throughout Orange County. The person who estimates your job stays your point of contact until it’s done.

Q: Do you haul away the debris?

A: Yes — haul-off is included in every demolition and removal estimate. Concrete, asphalt and metal go to recyclers whenever possible.

Q: Do you handle permits?

A: For jobs that need a demolition permit, we coordinate the permit, required asbestos survey and utility disconnect sign-offs, and tell you up front what your city requires.

Q: How soon can you start?

A: Small concrete and hauling jobs can often be scheduled within one to two weeks. Permitted demolition depends on the city’s permit timeline. Tell us your deadline when you request an estimate.

Q: Do you work with general contractors?

A: Yes. We work as a demolition subcontractor for GCs with written bids, clear scopes and reliable scheduling. Use our commercial bid form to send plans.

H2Residential Demolition done right

Most Orange County homes coming down today are 1950s–1970s tract houses being replaced with a larger custom build, or a single-story home losing part of its structure for a second-story addition. Both jobs look simple from the street and are not: there are gas and electric disconnects to confirm, a sewer lateral to cap, an asbestos survey the South Coast AQMD requires before any demolition, and neighbors on three sides who will notice dust, noise and trucks.

We plan each residential demolition around those realities. We walk the property with you, identify what is coming out and what has to stay — trees, block walls, a neighbor’s fence, a foundation you may want to reuse — and give you a written scope that your architect, builder and lender can all read.

  • Structure, foundation and flatwork removal as scoped
  • Separation of concrete, metal and green waste for recycling
  • Rough grade of the pad and final site clean-up

Our process

H2How we handle residential demolition

  • 01 Site walk & scope We measure the structure, note access for equipment and trucks, and flag anything that needs to be protected.
  • 02 Permits & surveys We coordinate the demolition permit, the asbestos survey and utility disconnects so nothing stalls the start date.
  • 03 Soft strip Interior materials, fixtures and anything salvageable come out first, which keeps the main demolition cleaner and lowers disposal costs.
  • 04 Structure demolition Equipment brings the structure down in a controlled sequence with water on the work face to keep dust down.
  • 05 Foundation & haul-off Slab, footings and flatwork are broken out, loaded and hauled; concrete is taken to a recycler, not a landfill.
  • 06 Final grade The lot is rough-graded and left ready for your surveyor, soils engineer or builder.

Q: Do I need a permit to demolish a house in Orange County?

A: Yes. Every city in Orange County requires a demolition permit for removing a house or other habitable structure, and most require proof of utility disconnects and an asbestos survey before the permit is issued. We coordinate this paperwork as part of the job.

Q: How long does a house demolition take?

A: The physical demolition of a typical single-family home usually takes a few working days. The full timeline — including the permit, utility disconnects and asbestos survey — is more often several weeks, which is why we recommend calling us as soon as your rebuild plans are underway.

Q: Can you keep the foundation for my new build?

A: Sometimes. If your engineer wants to reuse part of the existing foundation, we demolish the structure down to it and protect it. We will tell you on the site walk whether that is realistic.

Q: What happens to the debris?

Q: Will you protect my neighbor’s fence and landscaping?
