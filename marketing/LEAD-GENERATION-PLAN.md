# D.RAM Demolition — Lead Generation Plan

Goal: a steady flow of demolition, concrete-removal and hauling jobs from Orange County homeowners **and** repeat work from general contractors. Start with the free channels, then add paid ads once the basics are in place.

> **No one can promise a set number of leads or a fixed cost per lead.** Results depend on your photos, how fast you respond, your prices and the season. Start small, measure, and spend more on what books jobs.

---

## Phase 1 — Free foundations (weeks 1–2)

### 1. Google Business Profile (most important free source of leads)
1. Go to **business.google.com** → add **D.RAM Demolition & Hauling**.
2. Category: **Demolition contractor**. Add secondary categories only for work you do (e.g. *Concrete contractor*, *Debris removal service*, *Junk removal service*).
3. Choose **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** and **hide your address**. Add service areas: Fullerton, Anaheim, Orange, Santa Ana, Irvine, Huntington Beach and the other OC cities you serve (max 20).
4. Phone 714-872-6566. Website: `https://dramdemo.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
5. Hours, services (copy the 14 service names from the website), and **at least 10 photos** of real jobs.
6. Verify (Google may ask for a video of your truck/equipment and tools).
7. **Reviews:** after every job, text the customer your Google review link. 10+ real reviews makes a big difference. Reply to every one.
8. Post a job photo to your profile every week.

### 2. Facebook Page + Instagram Business account
1. Create a **Facebook Page**: D.RAM Demolition & Hauling, category *Demolition & Excavation Company*. Add phone, website, service area, hours, logo and cover photo.
2. Create an **Instagram** account (e.g. @dramdemo) → Settings → switch to **Professional → Business**.
3. Connect them in **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) so you can post to both at once and see all messages in one inbox.
4. Instagram bio: *Demolition · Concrete Removal · Hauling | Orange County | Free estimates 👇* and the link `dramdemo.com/estimate`.
5. Post the 5 ready-made graphics over the first two weeks (captions in `social/CAPTIONS.md`), then 2–3 posts a week from real jobs.

### 3. Other free listings (30 minutes each)
- **Nextdoor Business Page:** homeowners ask neighbors "who do you recommend for…?" constantly. Claim your page and ask happy customers to recommend you there.
- **Yelp** (free listing only; you don't need to buy Yelp ads), **Bing Places**, **Apple Business Connect**.
- Use the exact same name, phone and website everywhere.

## Phase 2 — Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads (start week 2–3)

### Setup (one time)
1. In **Meta Business Suite** → **Settings → Ad accounts** → create an ad account and add a payment method.
2. **Install the Meta Pixel on the website.** In **Events Manager**, create a Pixel and send Claude the **Pixel ID** (a long number). Claude will add it to the site and record a *Lead* every time someone submits an estimate request, so Meta can find more people like them.
3. Make sure your Page has the 5 posts up first. People check your page before they call.

### Your first campaign
| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | **Leads** |
| **Conversion location** | Start with **Instant forms** (the form opens inside Facebook/Instagram, easiest for people on phones). Later, test **Website** (sends people to dramdemo.com/estimate, fewer but better leads, and they can upload photos). |
| **Form type** | **Higher intent** (adds a review screen, which filters out accidental taps) |
| **Form questions** | Name, phone, ZIP code; *What do you need removed?* (Driveway/concrete, Patio, Garage/structure, Interior remodel demo, Block wall, Junk/debris, Other); *When do you need it done?* (ASAP, within 2 weeks, 1–3 months, just pricing) |
| **Location** | Orange County, CA (or the specific cities you want most), "People living in this location" |
| **Age** | 28–65+ |
| **Audience** | Let Meta's **Advantage+ audience** find people; don't over-narrow at first |
| **Placements** | Advantage+ placements (Facebook + Instagram feeds, Reels, Stories) |
| **Budget** | **$15–25/day** for the first 30 days (about $450–750). Don't judge results before ~2 weeks; Meta needs time to learn. |
| **Creative** | 3 ads in one ad set: graphic **01** (now booking), **05** (3 steps), and your best **before/after** or a **10–20 s video** of equipment working. Video usually wins. |

### Ad copy to start with
**Ad A — Driveway / concrete**
> Cracked driveway or old patio? We break it out, haul it away and leave a clean, level base for your new concrete or pavers. Free written estimates across Orange County.
> **Headline:** Concrete & Driveway Removal — Free Estimate · **Button:** Get quote

**Ad B — Remodel demo**
> Starting a kitchen or bathroom remodel? We handle the demo: cabinets, tile, flooring, drywall. Debris hauled out the same day. Homeowners & contractors, get a free estimate.
> **Headline:** Remodel Demo Done Right · **Button:** Get quote

**Ad C — Fast quote**
> Text us a few photos and get a written demolition price, with haul-off included. Garages, patios, walls, driveways, interiors and junk removal. Serving all of Orange County.
> **Headline:** Free Demolition Estimate in 3 Steps · **Button:** Get quote

### The part that matters most: speed
- Turn on **lead notifications** in Meta Business Suite (phone app) so every lead pings your phone.
- **Call or text within 5 minutes.** Leads contacted in the first few minutes book far more often than ones called hours later. Have a saved text ready: *"Hi [name], this is David with D.RAM Demolition — got your request for [service]. Can you text me a few photos of the area? I'll get you a price today."*
- Keep a simple sheet: date, name, service, source (Meta/Google/referral), quoted $, won/lost. After 30 days you'll know your real cost per job.

### When to change things
- After 2 weeks, turn off the ad with the highest cost per lead and add a new photo or video.
- If leads are cheap but low quality, switch to **Website** conversion or add more qualifying questions.
- If an ad is working, raise the budget 20–30% at a time, not all at once.

## Phase 3 — Google ads (when budget allows)

- **Google Search Ads** show your ad when people search *"concrete removal near me"*, *"demolition contractor Orange County"*, *"driveway removal Anaheim"*. These are people ready to hire. Start around **$20–30/day**, targeting the search terms above, sending clicks to the matching service page. Claude can set up conversion tracking for calls and forms (the website is already built for it).
- **Google Local Services Ads** (the "Google Screened" ads at the very top) are often the best leads for contractors, but Google requires **license and insurance verification** for most contractor categories. Plan on these once you're licensed and insured.

## Phase 4 — Offline & relationship leads (often the best jobs)

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| **Truck & trailer** | Magnetic signs or a wrap with logo, *Demolition · Concrete · Hauling*, phone and QR code. A truck parked on a job is advertising. |
| **Job-site yard sign** | With the homeowner's OK, put a sign out front during every job. Neighbors see the work happening. |
| **Door hangers** | Hang 20–30 on the street around each job: *"We're working at your neighbor's this week. Need anything torn out? Free estimate."* |
| **General contractors & remodelers** | Visit or call remodelers, ADU builders, pool builders, concrete/paver installers and landscapers. They all need a demo sub they can count on. Leave cards; follow up after a week. One good GC can mean steady work. |
| **Property managers & HOAs** | Trip-hazard sidewalks, tenant turnovers, block walls. Email the OC property-management companies with your services. |
| **Realtors** | Pre-listing clean-outs, junk removal, old sheds and patios. Hand cards to a few active local agents. |
| **Referral thank-you** | Give past customers and trade partners a thank-you (e.g. a gift card) for every referral that books. |
| **Bid platforms (commercial)** | Create free subcontractor profiles on **BuildingConnected** and **PlanHub** to get invited to bid on GC projects. Many commercial and public jobs require a license and insurance, so this grows once those are in place. |

## Weekly routine (about 2 hours a week)

- **Every job:** before / during / after photos from the same spot, ask for a Google review, door hangers on the street.
- **Mon:** post a before/after (Instagram + Facebook + Google Business Profile).
- **Wed:** post a short video or tip.
- **Fri:** check ads: cost per lead, which ad is winning; reply to every review.
- **Monthly:** send Claude new photos and projects to add to the website portfolio; review which channel brought the jobs that actually paid.
