# Google Business Profile — Setup Guide for D.RAM Demolition & Hauling

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is what shows up on **Google Maps** and in the **map results** when someone searches "demolition contractor near me" or "concrete removal Anaheim". For a local contractor it is usually the single biggest free source of calls.

> Google decides who ranks in the map results mainly on **relevance** (categories, services, description), **distance** (how close the searcher is to your business) and **prominence** (reviews, photos, activity, your website). No one can guarantee a top spot, but a complete, active profile with steady real reviews is what moves you up.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes)

- **Use a Google account you'll keep forever**, ideally one tied to your business (e.g. the account for david@dramdemo.com if that email runs on Google, or a dedicated Gmail like dramdemolition@gmail.com). Don't use a personal account you might lose.
- **Have ready:** logo, 10+ job photos, your real street address (it will be **hidden**, but Google needs it for verification), phone **714-872-2034**, hours.
- **Never use a P.O. box or virtual office address.** Google suspends listings for that. A home address is fine; it stays hidden.

## Step 1: Create the profile

1. Go to **business.google.com** → **Manage now** / **Add business** → sign in.
2. **Business name:** `D.RAM Demolition & Hauling`
   - Use your real business name **exactly**. Don't add keywords like "D.RAM Demolition – Concrete Removal Orange County". Google treats that as spam and can suspend the listing.
3. **Business category:** type and select **Demolition contractor** (this is your *primary* category, the most important setting on the whole profile).
4. **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"** → **No.** (You go to customers; this hides your address.)
5. **Service areas:** add **Orange County, CA** plus your key cities (up to 20 total), for example:
   Fullerton · Anaheim · Orange · Santa Ana · Irvine · Huntington Beach · Garden Grove · Costa Mesa · Placentia · Brea · Yorba Linda · Tustin · Buena Park · Newport Beach · Westminster · Fountain Valley · La Habra · Cypress · Lake Forest
6. **Phone:** `714-872-2034` · **Website:** paste exactly
   `https://dramdemo.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
   (the extra part lets Google Analytics show you which visitors came from your profile).
7. Google will ask for your **address for verification**. Enter your real address. It will not be shown publicly.

## Step 2: Verify (required before you appear on Maps)

Google picks the method; for service-area businesses it's usually a **video verification**. Record **one continuous video** (no cuts), roughly 1 minute:

1. **Where you are:** start outside showing a street sign or landmark that matches your area (Fullerton).
2. **That the business is real:** show your **work truck/trailer**, ideally with your logo (truck magnets help a lot), plus your equipment and tools.
3. **That you run it:** show something only the owner could: unlock the truck or toolbox, open your work equipment, show business paperwork or invoices with the business name.

Upload it through the profile and wait. Usually it takes a few days, sometimes longer. **Don't edit the business name, address or category while verification is pending**, as that can restart the review.

## Step 4: Photos (profiles with lots of real photos get far more calls)

Upload at least **10 to start**, then **2–3 new ones every week**:
- **Logo** (square version) and a **cover photo** (your best wide job photo or truck shot)
- Before/after pairs from real jobs. Name the files descriptively before uploading (e.g. `driveway-removal-anaheim.jpg`).
- Your **truck, trailer and equipment**
- **You and your crew** on a job (people trust faces)
- Short **videos** (10–30 s) of equipment working

Use your own photos only. Never use stock images.

## Step 5: Reviews (the biggest ranking + trust factor you control)

1. In your profile, tap **Ask for reviews** to get your **short review link**. Send it to Claude so it can be added to the website.
2. **Ask every happy customer, the day the job finishes**, by text:
   > "Thanks again for choosing D.RAM Demolition, [Name]! If you were happy with the work, a quick Google review would mean a lot to a small local business: [your review link]"
3. **Reply to every review** within a day or two, positive or negative. Mention the job and city naturally: *"Thanks, Maria! Glad the driveway removal in Fullerton went smoothly."*
4. **Rules (to avoid penalties):** never pay for reviews or offer discounts/gifts in exchange, never write your own or have family post fake ones, and ask everyone (not just people you think will be positive).

Goal: **10 reviews in the first 60 days**, then a steady few each month.

## Step 6: Stay active (weekly, 10 minutes)

- **Post an update every week** (Add update): a before/after photo + 2–3 sentences + "Call or text 714-872-2034" and a **Get quote** button linking to dramdemo.com/estimate. Example:
  > *Driveway removal in Anaheim — old cracked concrete broken out, hauled to recycling, and the base left ready for new pavers. Free written estimates across Orange County.*
- Add **new photos** weekly.
- **Answer calls and messages fast**. Google notices how responsive you are.
- Keep **hours and holiday hours** accurate.

## Step 7: Connect it to everything else

- Send Claude your **profile link** and **review link**. They get added to the website footer and Google data so Google connects the two.
- Use the **exact same name, phone and website** everywhere: website, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, Nextdoor, business cards.
- Once the site has Google Analytics, you'll see visits from your profile under *google / organic / gbp*.

## Things that get profiles suspended — avoid these

- Keywords or city names added to the business name
- P.O. boxes, virtual offices or a fake address
- Multiple profiles for the same business (e.g. one per city)
- Fake, paid or incentivized reviews
- A phone number or website that doesn't match the business
