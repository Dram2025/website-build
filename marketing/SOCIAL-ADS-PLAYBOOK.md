# D.RAM Demolition: Facebook, Instagram & Nextdoor Ads

Goal: **qualified estimate requests** from Orange County homeowners and contractors.
Phone: 714-872-2034 · Website: https://dramdemo.com · Privacy policy: https://dramdemo.com/privacy/

> Google Ads catches people already searching. Facebook, Instagram and Nextdoor put your work in front of people **before** they search: the homeowner who has been meaning to rip out that cracked driveway. These ads win on **real job photos, a clear offer and fast follow-up**.

## Part 1: Facebook + Instagram (Meta)

### Step 1. One-time setup (about 45 minutes, on a computer)

1. Go to **business.facebook.com** and create a **Business portfolio**: *D.RAM Demolition & Hauling*.
2. **Facebook Page:** name *D.RAM Demolition & Hauling*, category *Demolition & Excavation Company*. Add phone, website, service area (Orange County), hours, logo and cover photo.
3. **Instagram:** switch your account to a **Business** account and connect it to the Page (Business Suite → Settings → Instagram accounts).
4. **Ad account:** Settings → Accounts → Ad accounts → **Add → Create new**. Add a payment method.
5. **Meta Pixel:** Events Manager → **Connect data → Web** → name it *dramdemo.com*. Copy the **Pixel ID** (a long number) and send it to Claude. Claude adds it to the website so every estimate request counts as a *Lead* in Meta.
6. Install the **Meta Business Suite** app on your phone and turn on notifications for **Leads** and **Messages**.

### Step 2. Create the lead campaign

In **Ads Manager → + Create**:

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Campaign objective | **Leads** |
| Special ad category | **None.** Contractor ads are not "Housing" ads. |
| Budget | **$15–20/day** (Advantage campaign budget on) |
| Conversion location | **Instant forms** (the form opens inside Facebook/Instagram; easiest on phones) |
| Performance goal | Maximize number of leads |
| Location | People **living in** Orange County cities you serve, or a 15–20 mile radius around Fullerton |
| Age | 25–65+ |
| Audience | **Advantage+ audience**. Don't add interests; Meta finds buyers better on its own at this budget. |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements (Feeds, Reels, Stories) |

Add **3–5 ads** (different photos/videos and text). Meta automatically gives more budget to the best one.

### Step 3. Build the Instant Form

Form type: **Higher intent** (adds a review step, which cuts junk leads).

Questions:
1. What do you need removed or demolished? *(Concrete driveway / patio / slab · Interior remodel demo · Garage / shed / ADU site prep · Block or retaining wall · Debris hauling · Other)*
2. Approximate size? *(short answer, e.g. "20 × 30 ft driveway")*
3. When are you hoping to start? *(Within 2 weeks · 1–3 months · Just planning)*
4. I am a… *(Homeowner · Contractor · Property manager)*
5. City or ZIP
6. Name, phone, email (Meta pre-fills these)

- Privacy policy: `https://dramdemo.com/privacy/`
- Completion screen: *"Thanks! We'll call or text you within 1 business day. Want a faster price? Text photos to 714-872-2034."* Add a **Call** button.

### Step 4. Ads (photos/videos + text)

**What works best, in order:**
1. **Before/after photos** of the same spot from the same angle
2. **Short vertical videos (8–20 s):** breaking concrete, loading the truck, a quick walk-through of a finished site. Add bold captions; most people watch muted.
3. **You on camera (15–30 s):** "I'm David, owner of D.RAM Demolition in Fullerton…"

Keep license plates and house numbers out of frame.

Headline: **Driveway & Patio Removal: Free Estimates**

> Homeowners and contractors across Orange County.

Headline: **Remodel Demo & Strip-Outs in Orange County**

**Ad C: owner-led**
> Locally owned in Fullerton. You deal with the owner from estimate to clean-up.
> Concrete removal · Interior demo · Garage & ADU site prep · Block walls · Debris hauling
> Tap "Get quote" and tell us about your project.

Button: **Get quote**

### Step 5. After launch

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Don't change anything. Meta is learning. Answer every lead fast. |
| Day 7 | Pause any ad with lots of spend and zero leads. |
| Day 14 | Judge lead quality (real jobs?). Replace the weakest 1–2 ads with new photos/videos. |
| Day 30 | Compare cost per **booked job** with Google Ads; move budget to what books work. |
| Month 2+ | Add a **$5/day retargeting** ad set (people who watched your videos or visited dramdemo.com) using before/after videos. |

Rough expectation: demolition/concrete leads on Meta often cost around **$30–90** each in Orange County. It varies a lot with season and creative, and nobody can guarantee a number.

## Part 2: Nextdoor

Nextdoor is where neighbors ask "who do you recommend?", so it's strong for local contractors. Do the free part first; it makes the paid ads work better.

### Step 1. Free Business Page (do this first)

1. Download the **Nextdoor** app → menu → **Create a Business Page** (or go to business.nextdoor.com).
2. Business name *D.RAM Demolition & Hauling*, category *Demolition* / *Concrete* / *Hauling*, phone 714-872-2034, website https://dramdemo.com, service area: Fullerton and nearby cities.
3. Add your logo, a cover photo and **at least 5 job photos**.
4. **Get recommendations:** ask past customers, friends and neighbors who've seen your work to recommend you on Nextdoor (send them your Business Page link). Recommendations are the #1 trust signal on Nextdoor.
5. **Answer "looking for a contractor" posts** in your area from your Business Page: be helpful and short, never spammy.

### Step 2. Nextdoor ads

1. Go to **ads.nextdoor.com** (Nextdoor Ads Manager) and sign in with your Business Page account. Add a payment method.
2. **Create campaign:**
   - Objective: **Conversions** if you add the Nextdoor pixel (see below), otherwise **Clicks / Website traffic**
   - Landing page: `https://dramdemo.com/estimate/?utm_source=nextdoor&utm_medium=paid`
   - Targeting: pick **ZIP codes** around Fullerton (e.g. 92831–92835, Placentia 92870, Brea 92821, Yorba Linda 92886/92887, La Habra 90631, Anaheim Hills 92807/92808) and add more over time
   - Budget: **$10–15/day** to start
3. **Ad:** one strong before/after photo plus short, neighborly text, e.g.:
   > **Your neighbor in Fullerton for demolition and concrete removal.**
   > Driveways, patios, block walls, interior demo and hauling. Free written estimates. Send photos for a fast quote.

   Headline: **Free Demolition & Concrete Estimates**. Button: **Get quote**.
4. **Nextdoor pixel (optional, recommended):** in Ads Manager, create a pixel and send the ID to Claude to add to dramdemo.com, so Nextdoor can track estimate requests.

Menu names in Nextdoor change often; if something looks different, look for the closest match or send a screenshot.

## Part 3: Answer leads fast (this decides who gets the job)

- **Call or text every lead within 5–15 minutes** during business hours. If you can't call, text right away:
  > "Hi [Name], this is David with D.RAM Demolition. Got your request about [job]. Can you text me a couple of photos so I can get you a price? Or I can call, whatever's easier."
- **Facebook/Instagram DMs:** Business Suite → Inbox → Automations → turn on an **instant reply** with the same message.
- **Track every lead** in one sheet: date · source (Google / Facebook / Instagram / Nextdoor / referral) · job type · quote · won/lost · job $. After 30 days you'll know your real **cost per booked job** for each channel.

## Part 4: Free posting (3 times a week)

People check your Page before they call; an empty Page loses leads.

- **Mon:** before/after photo · **Wed:** short job video · **Fri:** tip or "now booking" post
- Post the same content to **Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor and your Google Business Profile**.
- Ready-made posts and captions: `marketing/social/`.

## Rules that keep your accounts safe

- Use **your own photos only**, no stock images or other companies' work.
- **No fake reviews or fake urgency.**
- Don't claim **licensed, insured or bonded** until you are.
- Don't mention personal traits in ads ("Are you a senior?"). Meta rejects them.
- Keep the **same name, phone and website** everywhere.
